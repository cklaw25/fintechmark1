# ClearLane: 1:30 demo video script (team LeHustle: Bao, Hassan, Memphis)

About 230 words, roughly 150 words per minute. [Brackets] = what's on screen.
Before recording: open the End-to-end tab, dark mode, 125% zoom. Run Case 2 before Case 3.

## Opening (0:00 – 0:08)
[End-to-end tab, Chidi intro box visible]
"Hi, I'm Memphis, with Bao and Hassan, team LeHustle. We built ClearLane: AI that checks cross-border payments in seconds, not weeks."

## The person (0:08 – 0:18)
"Meet Chidi. He runs a print studio in Lagos. His London client's last £1,200 payment sat nine days in manual checks, and he couldn't pay his staff."

## Why now (0:18 – 0:28)
"Until now, a person had to read every invoice and clear every false alarm by hand. Now AI can read the documents, compare them with payment history, and explain its decision in seconds."

## The demo (0:28 – 0:45)
[Case 1 → tap Send. Checks tick in, sender phone shows Sent, Chidi's phone gets the credit alert]
"Here's ClearLane inside Northgate Bank. The client taps send. ClearLane checks the account name, the invoice, sanctions and payment history. Cleared in eight seconds, and Chidi gets paid. Eight seconds, not nine days."

## The wow (0:45 – 1:03)
[Case 2 → Send → "Payment paused". Point at the fake email address]
"Now a scammer emails a fake invoice with new bank details. ClearLane catches the changed account, the wrong name and the lookalike email address. Payment paused; the money never leaves."
[Case 3 → Send → "This account was used in a scam…"]
"When the same scammer comes back under a new name, our flagged database catches them on the first check."

## Trust (1:03 – 1:18)
[Flagged database tab, then Analyst queue]
"ClearLane never releases a flagged payment on its own. A person decides, every decision is logged with its evidence, and victims are protected, never flagged. Real: the flow and the database logic. Mocked: the bank, the data and the AI responses."

## Close (1:18 – 1:30)
[Back to End-to-end, Case 1 result: "8.2 sec vs 9 days"]
"Banks pay per payment checked: fewer analyst hours, fewer fraud refunds. One bank today, a shared scam network across banks tomorrow. We're LeHustle, and this is ClearLane."
